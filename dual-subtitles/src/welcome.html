Watch in two languages at once

Dual Subtitles shows the video's original subtitles and your own language together on YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+. On any other site, or for a video without subtitles, it can generate them from the audio.

¿Dónde estabas anoche?

Where were you last night?
  1. Click the extension icon and choose My language.
  2. Play a video. If it has subtitles, both languages appear at the bottom.
  3. No subtitles, or a different site? Click the icon and press Listen to this tab, or press Alt+Shift+S. Speech is recognised on your computer. The first time, it downloads a speech model.
